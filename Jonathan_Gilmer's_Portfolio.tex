\noindent
\portfolioimage{tractor_original.jpg}{Abandoned tractor I started with}{0.24\textwidth}{0.98in}\hfill

\begin{projectbullets}
  \item Converted an abandoned ride-on tractor into a \textbf{6-speed manual go-kart} with a \textbf{26 hp} motor connected by a belt drive
  \item I fabricated a gas pedal, clutch, shifting linkage, mount for the new motor, and a smaller rear transaxle pulley to increase top speed
  \item The completed build shifted cleanly through all the gears and increased top speed from 8 mph to 34 mph
\end{projectbullets}
